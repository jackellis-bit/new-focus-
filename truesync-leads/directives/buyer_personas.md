# TrueSync Buyer Personas

> Knowledge base for TrueSync sales targeting. Defines who our buyers are, what they care about, and what they need from us.

## Who They Are

### Primary Decision Makers

**International Originals / Local Originals Leadership**
- Commissioning and greenlight influence
- Found at: Netflix, Amazon, WBD/Max, Peacock
- They decide which non-English titles get English localization investment

**Content Acquisitions Leadership**
- Buyers who need imported titles to win in English-speaking markets
- Found at: Netflix, Amazon, WBD, Paramount
- They own relationships with foreign studios and performance narratives

**Distribution / Global Content Distribution**
- Territory strategy, release strategy, monetization
- Found at: Sony, Lionsgate, Paramount, Universal, WBD
- They sell international titles into English-speaking markets

**Content Strategy / Programming**
- Portfolio performance, slate optimization, platform priorities
- Found at: All major platforms, especially AVOD (Tubi, Pluto, Roku)
- Minutes watched = revenue, localization quality affects RPMs

### Secondary Decision Makers (Influencers/Champions)

**Consumer Insights / Data Science**
- Validation partners, internal champions for measurable uplift
- They provide the data that justifies localization investments
- Key to proving ROI and scaling from pilot to slate commitment

**Studio Business Owners (Label/Franchise)**
- Performance, brand, and critical reception matter
- Own specific franchises or studio labels
- Care about quality and brand consistency across markets

## What They Care About

### Near-term ROI (12-18 months)
- Lift in starts (viewers beginning content)
- Minutes viewed (engagement depth)
- Completion rates (content satisfaction)
- Retention impact (subscriber/viewer loyalty)
- PVOD/theatrical conversions (revenue impact)

### Global Scalability
- Repeatable deployment across slates, not one-off experiments
- Template-able workflows that don't require reinvention per title
- Predictable timelines and quality levels

### Competitive Edge
- Making international titles feel native in English-speaking markets
- Breaking the "subtitle barrier" that limits audience size
- Capturing audiences who skip non-English content entirely

### Reputational Safety
- Rights/consent clarity (no legal exposure)
- Security posture (content protection)
- "Assistive" positioning (enhancing original, not replacing)
- Creator/talent alignment

## What They Need From Us

### Credible Data Layer
- Owned-slate performance insights showing TrueSync impact
- Independent research validating dubbing ROI
- Case studies with real metrics (not just testimonials)
- Competitive benchmarking data

### PoC Factory
- Fast pilot turnaround (days/weeks, not months)
- Reliable quality that doesn't require hand-holding
- Minimal internal bandwidth required from their team
- Clear success criteria defined upfront

### Clear Commercial Packaging
- Pilots that turn into slate commitments (not endless tests)
- Volume pricing that makes scale economical
- Flexible models: per-title, per-slate, platform-wide
- Integration options with existing workflows

## Objection Handling

### "We have existing dubbing vendors"
- TrueSync is additive, not replacement
- Focus on quality delta and speed, not vendor displacement
- Offer A/B test on performance metrics

### "Dubbing quality concerns"
- "Assistive" technology preserves original performance
- Creator/talent approval built into workflow
- Share quality comparison samples

### "Budget constraints"
- Start with high-ROI catalog titles (proven performers)
- Volume economics make slate deals economical
- ROI case studies show payback period

### "Internal process complexity"
- Minimal internal bandwidth required
- Integration with existing MAM/DAM workflows
- Dedicated success management
